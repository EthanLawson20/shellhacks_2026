See beyond the horizon with PHASE.

Phase is a network of microcontrollers that combines Wi-Fi sensing and audio analysis to help rescuers identify possible signs of life. Explore the following interactive simulation to see how its network works.

02NETWORK ARCHITECTURE

How to deploy

  1. 1

    Place the network

    Position one sender and three receiving nodes around the area. The nodes report their observations to a central gateway.

    1 sender, 3 receivers, 1 gateway
  2. 2

    Sense and listen

    Each node observes changes in the sender's Wi-Fi signal through channel state information (CSI). Node microphones provide audio samples for a model designed to flag possible human screams.

    Wi-Fi CSI + audio classification
  3. 3

    Review the room view

    The gateway brings the observations together for a display of possible areas of activity that responders can assess and verify on site.

    Estimated activity, human verification
03ML ARCHITECTURE

Recognizing signals that may need attention.

Wi-Fi CSI is compared with a room baseline to identify changes in movement. The audio network shown below is designed to score short microphone samples for patterns associated with human screams.

04USER CASE

In a rescue, every second spent searching the wrong spot can cost a life.

01

Sees through smoke and debris

Our star network is designed to detect movement and breathing where cameras go blind, even when survivors cannot call for help.

02

Scans the whole scene at once

Instead of one radar checking one spot at a time, the receiving nodes monitor their zones simultaneously and report to a central gateway.

03

Low cost, deployed in minutes

Built from low-cost nodes, the system is designed for rapid placement and calibration, giving responders a live view of where to search first.
